**“Projecting Gravitational Fluctuations onto Near-Horizon Throats,”**
arXiv:2608.00363 [hep-th] (2026).

- Abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00363
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.00363

## Equations/sections used directly by the numerical experiments

- Heun equation in Schrödinger form: eq. (2.1), repeated as eqs. (3.1)-(3.2).
- Regular large-`t` parameter expansion: eq. (2.11); for the isospectral construction, eqs. (3.3)-(3.4).
- Outer hypergeometric projection: Sec. 2.1, especially eqs. (2.4)-(2.5).
- Inner coordinate and hypergeometric projection: Sec. 2.3, especially eqs. (2.49)-(2.54).
- Matching domain: eq. (2.56), `1 << z << t` (with the numerical contour represented by `|z|`).
- First outer diffeomorphism: eqs. (3.19)-(3.21).
- First inner diffeomorphism: eqs. (3.23)-(3.24).
- Smooth inner/outer matching of the diffeomorphisms: eq. (3.26).
- Wave-function reconstruction: Sec. 3.5, especially eq. (3.41).
- Kerr application: Sec. 4 and Appendix D.
- Discussion/future numerical test of the `O(1/t)` wave-function correction: Sec. 5.
